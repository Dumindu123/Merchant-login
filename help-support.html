Q&A

Quick help

Answers to common questions.

How do I book a parcel?

Open New Parcel, enter the recipient and parcel details, then submit the booking. Your tracking number will appear after the parcel is created.

When will COD be settled?

COD is settled after delivery and reconciliation. View your balance and payout activity in COD & Wallet or check completed payouts in Settlements.

How can I request a return?

Send us a support request with the tracking number and the reason for return. Our operations team will review the request and contact you.

How do I update pickup details?

Update your business and pickup address in Store Profile, then save your changes.
